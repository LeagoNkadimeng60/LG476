.1 Website Project Proposal: MNL Tyres 

Project OVERVIEW 

The proposed website project is for MNL Tyres , a small automotive business that sells tyres and provides tyre- related services to individuals and businesses, The purpose of the website to establish profesional online presence for MNL Tyres and provide customers with convenient access to information about the business, its products and its services. 

Purpose and Objectives  

The main objective of the website is to help MNL Tyres attract new customers while providing existing customers with quick and convenient access to important business information. The website will display the different tyre brands, sizes, and categories available from the business. 

It will also provide information about services such as tyre fitting, puncture repairs, wheel balancing, and tyre inspections. Customers will be able to enquie about tyre prices and availability, request, quotations and make appointment enquiries, in addition, the website will display the business location, contact details and operating hours. 

Target Audience 

The target audience will include private vehicle owners, taxi operators, delivery drivers, small businesses with company vehicles, fleet owners, and motorists who require tyre replacements or repairs. The website will be designed to be simple and accessible so that customers can quickly find the information they need. 

Proposed Features and Design  

The website will contain Home, About Us, Our Products, Our Services, Special Offers and Contact us page. The home page will provide an introduction to MNL Tyres and highlight its main services. The products page will display available tyre brands, sizes, and categories. The Services page will explain the tyre related services offered. 

The website will have a professional automotive design using clear headings, high-quality tyre and vehicle images, simple navigation and a responsive layout suitable for mobile phones, tablets and computers. A contact form will allow customers to submit enquiries.  

 

Expected Outcome 

The website will be developed using HTML web development platform. The project will include planning, content creation, interface design, development, testing and final improvements. The success of the website will be measured by its ease of navigation, responsive design, and professional appearance. The completed website will provide MNL Tyres with a professional digital presence and make it easier for customers to find products, services, and contact information. The website will improve communication with customers, increase visibility, and potentially generate more enquiries and sales. 

Timeline and milestones 

The MNL Tyres website project will be completed over a four-week period. The timeline allows sufficient time for research , planning, design, development, testing and final submission. The final week includes time for corrections and possible delays.
